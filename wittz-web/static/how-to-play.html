How to Play Wittz

Wittz is a fast-paced party word game for 3–12 players. Get a prompt, write the funniest phrase, and vote for the best — playable free in your browser or on iPhone. Here's everything you need to know in 60 seconds.

The rules

1

Join or create a room

Create a room and share the 6-digit code or invite link, or join a friend's game. You need at least 3 players; up to 12 can play.

2

Read the prompt

Every round opens with a prompt — anything from "What's the worst advice you could give a time traveler?" to "My toxic trait is ______".

3

Write your phrase

You have about 25 seconds to write the funniest answer you can, up to 200 characters. Speed matters — but wit matters more.

4

Vote for the best

Everyone's answers appear anonymously. Vote for your favorite. You can't vote for your own, and only players who submitted can vote.

5

Score points and win

The round winner keeps their votes plus a 2-vote bonus. First player to 20 votes wins the game.

⭐ Stars: when a winning phrase earns 4 or more votes in a single round, it gets permanently starred and showcased in the community gallery.

Tips for winning

  • Subvert the prompt. The obvious answer never wins — the twist on the obvious answer does.
  • Short beats long. Punchlines land harder in ten words than in forty.
  • Know your room. Inside jokes crush with friends; absurdist one-liners win with strangers.
  • Submit early, edit later. You can update your answer until the timer runs out.
  • Play ranked. Once you've got the rhythm, ranked lobbies and the global leaderboard are where reputations are made.

Game modes

Casual rooms are for playing with friends — create a private room and share the invite link, or browse public lobbies. Ranked lobbies pit you against the community with an ELO-style rating and seasonal leaderboards. Groups let you keep a standing crew with its own games and stats.
