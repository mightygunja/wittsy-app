Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Wittz, the free party word game you can play in your browser or on iPhone.

What is Wittz?

Wittz is a fast-paced party word game for 3–12 players. Each round you get a prompt, write the funniest phrase you can in about 25 seconds, and everyone votes anonymously for the best answer. The round winner banks their votes plus a bonus — first to 20 votes wins. Read the full rules on the How to Play page.

Is Wittz free?

Yes — free to play with no ads. Optional in-app purchases on iPhone unlock cosmetic avatar items, coins, and the premium battle pass. None of them affect gameplay.

How many players do I need?

At least 3, up to 12. Rounds with fewer than 3 submissions are skipped automatically, so the game stays fair.

Can I play in my browser?

Yes — the full game runs free at wittz.app, no download or signup required. The iPhone app connects to the same games, so browser and app players share every room.

How do invite links work?

Every room has a 6-digit code and a share link like wittz.app/game/ABC123. Friends with the iPhone app jump straight into your room; friends without it play instantly in their browser.

What is ranked mode?

Ranked lobbies match you against the community with an ELO-style rating, seasonal leaderboards, and quick-match auto-matchmaking. Casual games with friends never affect your rating.

What are starred phrases?

When a winning phrase earns 4+ votes in one round, it's permanently starred and showcased in the community gallery.

How do I report inappropriate content?

Every phrase in the community gallery has a ⚑ Report link. Reported content is hidden for you immediately and reviewed by the moderation team. Explicit content is also filtered automatically before it can appear publicly.

Do I need an account?

No — tap Play Free and you're in as a guest instantly. Creating a free account later saves your progress, stats, avatar, and starred phrases across devices.

How do I delete my account and data?

Delete your account from Settings in the app, or follow the steps on the data deletion page.
